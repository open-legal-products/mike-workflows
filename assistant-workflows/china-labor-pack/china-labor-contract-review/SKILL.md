# China Labor Contract Review

PRC labor law is largely mandatory: many clauses that would be ordinary negotiated terms elsewhere are void or expose the employer to statutory liability. This workflow reviews the contract text against those rules from the side of the represented party. It does not replace the general Employment Agreement Review, and it does not run the PIPL process duties covered by the China PIPL Pack.

Confirm every cited article against the official Chinese text in force. A search hit is not verification. Points that are not confirmed in-session stay tagged [TO VERIFY] and cannot support a finding that a clause is lawful.

## Step 1 - Confirm before reviewing

1. Which side the user represents: employer or employee. If unclear, ask. Do not assume.
2. Place of work (province and city). Minimum wage, social-insurance base and some non-compete practice vary locally. If not given, add an "Information gap: place of work" row.
3. Contract type: fixed term, open-ended, or for the completion of a specific task. Dispatch labor, part-time work, internships, service (labor-service) contracts and rehired retirees are out of scope; say so and stop.
4. Facts outside the text, if the user has them: actual start date of work, number of fixed-term contracts already concluded, years of continuous service, total years of working life (for annual leave). Do not draw conclusions from facts you do not have; list them as information gaps.

## Step 2 - Risk scale (stated per side)

Red line means "do not sign or use without the change" on both sides, but the reason differs:

| Rating | Representing the employee | Representing the employer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red line | The clause breaches a mandatory rule, excludes or reduces a statutory right of the employee, or imposes an obligation the law does not allow (penalty, deposit, guarantee). | The clause breaches a mandatory rule and is likely void or unenforceable, or exposes the employer to statutory compensation, double wages or administrative liability. |
| High | Clearly unfavorable and outside the acceptable fallback, but lawful or curable. Must be negotiated. | A quantifiable legal exposure or enforcement risk that needs a drafting change or a supporting procedure. |
| Medium | Short of the standard position but within the acceptable fallback. | Same. |
| Low | Wording, numbering, cross-reference or naming issue; information-gap rows. | Same. |

Deviation type: Missing / Weaker than standard / Beyond acceptable fallback / Red line / Drafting error.

## Step 3 - Checklist

Apply only the represented side's position. Where a rating differs by side, it is stated as "employee: X; employer: Y".

1. **Parties and mandatory terms.** Required terms present: term, job and place of work, working time and rest, remuneration, social insurance, labor protection (Labor Contract Law (劳动合同法) Art. 17). Missing remuneration, job or term: red line. Inconsistent party names: low; high if the employer cannot be identified.
2. **Term and signing date.** A written contract must be concluded within one month from the start of employment (Labor Contract Law Art. 10). After one month and before one year without a written contract the employer pays double wages monthly (Art. 82; SPC Labor Dispute Interpretation (II) (最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（二）, Fa Shi [2025] No. 12) Arts. 6-7). After one full year the parties are deemed to have an open-ended contract (Art. 14), and double wages are not supported for that deemed period (Interpretation (II) Art. 9).
   - Blank contract, or contract start date later than the actual start of work: red line.
   - Signing date more than one month after the start of employment: employer: red line, Missing - a double-wage exposure has arisen (Art. 82) and the text cannot cure it; confirm the actual start date and size the exposure. The one-month period runs from the actual start of work (Art. 10). If the contract states a first day of work (for example a reporting or onboarding date), use it. Otherwise a stated start date earlier than the signing date is normally the employer's own record of that start, so rate on it unless the user gives a different actual start date. If the contract states different dates and does not show which is the first day of work, treat it as an information gap. Employee: medium, Drafting error - the start date is back-dated before signing and may be used to resist a double-wage claim; this is a rights notice, not a clause to strike. Suggest recording the true signing date and keeping evidence of the start and signing dates.
   - Start of employment cannot be determined from the text or the user's facts: information gap (low). Do not calculate double wages or test open-ended-contract triggers in this version.
3. **Probation.** Caps (Labor Contract Law Art. 19): term of three months to under one year, at most one month; one to under three years, at most two months; three years or more, or open-ended, at most six months. Only one probation with the same employer; none for task-based contracts or terms under three months. Exceeding a cap, a second probation, or a prohibited probation: red line on both sides. Probation already served beyond the cap triggers compensation (Art. 83). Termination during probation is limited (Art. 21). Vague hiring criteria: high.
4. **Probation pay.** Statutory floor (Labor Contract Law Art. 20; Implementing Regulations (劳动合同法实施条例) Art. 15): probation pay must be at least the local minimum wage, and must meet the 80 percent test, which both provisions state as alternatives (Art. 20: the lowest same-post wage or 80 percent of the agreed wage; Art. 15: 80 percent of the lowest same-post wage or 80 percent of the agreed wage). Below the local minimum, or failing every alternative: red line on both sides. This workflow's standard position is stricter: at least 80 percent of the agreed wage and at least the full lowest same-post wage. Pay that meets the statutory floor but not this position is not a statutory breach: employee: medium, Deviation from standard position; employer: low. Lowest same-post wage not stated in the contract: information gap (low); if pay is below 80 percent of the agreed wage and the same-post wage is unknown, ask for it before rating.
5. **Job, place and unilateral changes.** Amendment requires mutual agreement in writing (Labor Contract Law Art. 35). Reassignment after incompetence is a statutory step (Art. 40). Employer may unilaterally cut pay or move the employee to another city: red line on both sides (for the employer, the clause is likely unenforceable and acting on it invites resignation with severance). Unilateral reassignment with no conditions: high. Reassignment conditioned on business need, reasonableness, suitability to ability, no pay cut and same city: employee: medium; employer: standard position. Local court practice on "reasonable reassignment" varies [TO VERIFY].
6. **Remuneration and working time.** Unilateral pay cut: red line. Standard hours: compare a standard-hours contract against 8 hours a day and 40 a week (State Council Provisions on Working Hours of Employees (国务院关于职工工作时间的规定) Art. 3). Labor Law (劳动法) Art. 36 still states 44 hours a week on average, but Ministry of Labour guidance (劳部发〔1995〕187号, Q9) states that from 1 May 1997 overtime is calculated on a 40-hour week. Ordinary hours over 8 a day or 40 a week under the standard-hours system that the contract does not treat as overtime with overtime pay: high. Overtime requires consultation with the union and the employee and is capped at normally 1 hour a day, or 3 hours a day and 36 hours a month for special reasons (Labor Law Art. 41). Hours above 40 a week that are paid as overtime and stay within those limits: medium, a point to negotiate or clarify. A schedule or term that requires overtime beyond those limits, paid or not: red line on both sides. Overtime pay at least 150 percent, rest days without time off in lieu 200 percent, statutory holidays 300 percent (Labor Law Art. 44). Waiver of overtime pay or an all-inclusive wage that excludes it: red line. Special working-hour systems need approval [TO VERIFY: approval basis].
7. **Annual leave.** 5 days after one full year of total working life, 10 days after ten years, 15 days after twenty years (Regulations on Paid Annual Leave for Employees (职工带薪年休假条例) Art. 3). Below the statutory days or waiver: red line, after confirming total working years; otherwise information gap.
8. **Social insurance.** Mandatory (Labor Law Art. 72); register within 30 days of the start of employment (Social Insurance Law (社会保险法) Art. 58). An agreement or promise not to pay is void, and the employee may resign and claim severance (Interpretation (II) Art. 19; Labor Contract Law Art. 38). No contributions, none during probation, or a waiver: red line on both sides. Contract states the minimum base or a base below actual wages, or contributions only after probation: high. Base not stated: information gap.
9. **Workplace rules.** Rules on matters directly affecting employees must go through the democratic procedure and be published or notified (Labor Contract Law Art. 4). Rules can change pay or job unilaterally: employee: red line. General incorporation of "all current and future rules": employee: medium; employer: high, because rules not adopted and published lawfully cannot be relied on; add a list of published rules and a signed acknowledgement.
10. **Training and service period.** A service period and penalty are allowed only for employer-funded specialized technical training; the penalty may not exceed the training cost or the unperformed portion pro rata (Labor Contract Law Art. 22). Training costs are those with receipts plus related travel and direct costs (Implementing Regulations Art. 16). Resignation under Art. 38 for employer fault is not a breach of the service period (Implementing Regulations Art. 26). For special benefits such as housing, the court weighs actual loss, fault and years served (Interpretation (II) Art. 12).
    - Service period or penalty for onboarding or routine training, or penalty payable on resignation for employer fault: red line.
    - Penalty above actual cost or not pro-rated: high; give pro-rata replacement wording. The amount above either Art. 22 cap cannot be claimed, but the service-period agreement itself remains valid and the defect is curable by redrafting, so this workflow rates it high rather than red line.
    - Costs and receipts not listed: medium. Fixed repayment amount for special benefits: medium.
11. **Penalties, deductions, deposits and documents.** Penalties may be agreed only for service periods and non-competes (Labor Contract Law Art. 25). The employer may not hold identity documents, require a guarantee or collect property (Art. 9). Clauses excluding the employee's rights or exempting the employer's statutory liability are invalid (Art. 26). Any other penalty, including disguised penalties framed as liquidated damages, wage deductions or repayment of all benefits, and any deposit, guarantee or retained document: red line on both sides.
12. **Confidentiality.** A penalty attached to confidentiality alone is not permitted (Labor Contract Law Arts. 23 and 25): red line on both sides; for the employer, the clause is unenforceable. Scope should be trade secrets and IP-related confidential matters.
13. **Non-compete.** Only senior managers, senior technical staff and others with confidentiality duties; at most two years after termination; monthly compensation (Labor Contract Law Arts. 23-24). If no compensation is agreed and the employee complies, 30 percent of the prior twelve-month average wage, not below the local minimum wage (SPC Labor Dispute Interpretation (I) (最高人民法院关于审理劳动争议案件适用法律问题的解释（一）, Fa Shi [2020] No. 26) Art. 36). A non-compete is ineffective for an employee with no access to trade secrets, and excess scope is void (Interpretation (II) Art. 13). Over two years, or no access to secrets: red line. A clause expressly excluding or waiving compensation: red line (Art. 26). Compensation simply not agreed does not by itself make the restriction ineffective: if the employee complies, they may claim the Interpretation (I) Art. 36 amount monthly. Rate an omitted amount high on both sides (employee: a binding restriction with pay left to a claim; employer: default 30 percent exposure) and propose an express monthly amount. Local compensation practice [TO VERIFY].
14. **Termination and severance.** Employee may resign on 30 days' written notice, 3 days during probation (Labor Contract Law Art. 37). Employer termination only on statutory grounds (Arts. 39-41; probation limits in Art. 21). Economic layoffs under Art. 41 are lawful only where one of its four grounds exists (reorganization under the Enterprise Bankruptcy Law; serious difficulty in production and operation; a change of products, major technological innovation or adjustment of business methods where layoffs are still needed after the contracts have been amended; or another major change in the objective economic circumstances on which the contracts were based that makes them impossible to perform) and its procedure is followed: the employer is laying off 20 or more employees, or fewer than 20 but at least 10 percent of its total workforce; it explains the situation to the union or all employees 30 days in advance and hears their opinions; and it reports the layoff plan to the labor administration. Art. 41 also gives listed employees priority to be retained, and those laid off notice and priority if the employer rehires within six months. Art. 42 bars termination under Arts. 40-41 for protected employees (for example during pregnancy, maternity or nursing, or the medical treatment period). A clause that expressly allows layoffs or dismissal for any business reason outside the Art. 41 grounds: red line on both sides, as a non-statutory dismissal ground. A layoff clause that is silent on or omits these grounds or conditions: high; add them. Severance of one month's wage per year of service (Art. 47; Art. 46 for when payable); unlawful termination compensation (Art. 87). Longer notice, approval requirement for resignation, non-statutory dismissal grounds such as forced ranking, or advance waiver of severance: red line. Separation agreements are generally valid but can be rescinded for gross misunderstanding or manifest unfairness (Interpretation (I) Art. 35).
15. **Employee personal information.** Processing necessary for HR management under lawful rules and collective contracts does not need consent (Personal Information Protection Law (个人信息保护法) Art. 13). Blanket consent to "all information the employer considers necessary", background checks or monitoring without a stated scope: medium. Blanket consent covering sensitive personal information or third-party disclosure: [TO VERIFY: separate-consent articles]. For impact assessments and other PIPL process duties, use the China PIPL Pack.
16. **Dispute resolution.** Labor disputes go to labor dispute arbitration first; a party may sue in court only after the award, except as that law provides (Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law (劳动争议调解仲裁法) Arts. 2 and 5). Commercial arbitration, direct court jurisdiction or a bar on labor arbitration: red line.
17. **Drafting.** Party names, definitions, numbering, cross-references and schedules: low, unless a core term becomes uncertain.

## Output

One Markdown table, one row per substantive issue, final row "Overall risk". Columns:

| Issue | Clause and text | Risk | Deviation type | Authority | Verification | Replacement wording | If refused |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Clause and text: quote the key words and give the clause number; write "Not agreed" if absent.
- Authority: statute short name and article, with the Chinese name in parentheses on first use.
- Verification: "Verified" (official Chinese text read in this session), "Checklist" (same as this checklist, not re-read), or "[TO VERIFY]".
- Replacement wording: paste-ready clause text from the represented side, minimal change. If the contract is in Chinese, draft the wording in Chinese.
- If refused: acceptable fallback, escalation point or alternative protection.
- Information gaps get their own rows rated low, with the fact needed.

## Governance boundary

The workflow drafts review comments and wording. It does not give a legal opinion. Whether to sign, how to negotiate and whether to send anything stay with a qualified PRC lawyer or the client.
