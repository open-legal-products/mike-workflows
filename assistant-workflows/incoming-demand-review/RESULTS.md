# Incoming Demand Review 0.1.2 — bounded pilot

**Disposition: a useful experimental internal add-on for review.** The observed
gain is narrower than general superiority: repeated reconciliation and
receipt/basis follow-ups on one development case, plus a corrected deadline
conclusion on one regression case. Strong generic answers already handled the
other two cases well. Legal accuracy, production reliability and lawyer time
saved have not been established.

Author: Lawyered. Tests completed 2026-11-02. All documents and parties are fictional.

## What changed for the reviewer

The generic PH27 answers already caught the deposit contradiction, disputed
acceptance, unsupported extra and missing invoice. They did not request the full
payment/account reconciliation or establish how receipt and the asserted date's
basis should be checked. Both final workflow answers add those tasks while
retaining the supplier's completed-delivery position and the buyer's defect report.

In the more complex Alder record, the generic answer identified the payment
allocation/reversal and most other issues correctly. It nevertheless declared a
receipt-triggered contractual window expired while expressly acknowledging that
receipt was unproved. The final workflow leaves the trigger/start and expiry
unestablished. This is one observed correction, not a repeated cross-case defect.

The workflow also handles genuine acceptance, approved extras and complete
accounts. It does not obtain a higher material-finding score on Nightjar or the
fresh Tideglass case: the generic answers were already strong there.

## Independently reviewed findings

An independent agent reviewed whole outputs against frozen source-grounded
checks, including qualifications, adverse facts, arithmetic, source entailment
and every saved quote/version target. This was not a human lawyer validation or
an automated keyword score. Readable source/location references received credit
even when a native citation artifact was missing. Tables and verbosity earned
no bonus. Different cases' denominators must not be pooled into one percentage.

| Case and status for final revision | Strongest generic control | Final workflow | Supported conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PH27 development, two repetitions | 8/10, 8/10 | 10/10, 10/10 | Adds account reconciliation and receipt/basis follow-ups in both repetitions; core dispute analysis already existed. |
| Nightjar regression | 7/7 | 7/7 | Both credit real acceptance/approved extra and separate supported from unsupported charges. |
| Alder regression | 8/9 | 9/9 | Avoids the control's unsupported expiry conclusion; retains complex payment/adverse-inspection evidence. |
| Tideglass fresh post-freeze case, one matched pair | 8/8 | 8/8 | Both honor genuine acceptance, storage, credit, extension and confirmed receipt; no added missed material finding. |

PH27's missing-material and next-question checks overlap. Do not market two
rubric cells as several independent discoveries. The stronger original ambition
of demonstrating broad comparative gains or two independent new findings across
cases is **not established** by these results. The evidence supports a focused
internal review aid worth maintainer and lawyer review.

Tideglass was independently authored and frozen after version 0.1.2 was frozen,
without inspecting its outputs. Its key was withheld from the generating model
and candidate author until both final trials completed. Nightjar/Alder initially
served as held-outs for 0.1.1, then became regression cases after the observed
failure below. They are not relabelled as fresh tests of 0.1.2. Source text and
substantive review keys remained unchanged.

## Failures retained

1. A stock Compare Documents turn at 8,192 stopped for length mid-citation block.
   Raw text, parser and SSE evidence identify that specific failure. It was not
   scored as an automatic semantic traceability failure. All stock conditions
   were subsequently repeated at the shared 16,384 allowance.
2. Version 0.1.0 scored 9/10 in both PH27 trials because it omitted the supplier's
   express delivery-complete position. Version 0.1.1 added a general instruction
   to retain each side's completion/performance account.
3. Version 0.1.1 exhausted 16,384 output tokens on Alder while writing citations;
   its native citation array was empty. The generic control completed. This was
   not a clean held-out pass.
4. Version 0.1.2 added concise-output discipline without raising the allowance.
   Alder then completed normally: final step 8,073 output tokens, ten native
   citations. One successful regression does not guarantee completion on larger
   files. The soft 600–900-word target is sometimes exceeded; PH27 final prose
   was approximately 1,000–1,100 whitespace-separated words.

An early exploratory generic answer also lacked saved native citations, but no
raw stream was captured for that turn; its cause remains unknown. The later
observed truncations do not prove what caused it.

## Known limits and small imperfections

The final Tideglass candidate misleadingly describes the supplier account as
having “no stated author or date,” although it also correctly quotes its
complete-project scope and dated cut-off. It should distinguish a missing named
preparer from the stated origin/cut-off. This did not change the balance or
deadline result, but it needs editing. Some verification requests are more
cautious than the already strong documentary record requires. The generic
answer also makes speculative completeness requests. Passing material checks
does not mean flawless prose or a filing-ready legal opinion.

Four short fictional packets, two PH27 repetitions and one fresh final pair
cannot establish broad reliability across real demands, jurisdictions, scanned
records or larger document sets. No randomized lawyer review/edit-time study
was performed. Token counts and generation latency do not establish time saved.
No confidentiality, security, complete-action-isolation or externally safe output
guarantee is claimed. Publication, maintainer acceptance and production use are
separate decisions.

## Cost and reproducible checks

Across all 28 captured top-level turns, including exploratory/failed revisions:
872,710 reported main input tokens and 181,971 main output tokens. At the checked
Sonnet 5.5 standard rates, the main-usage estimate is **USD3.56513**. Treating all
reported input as five-minute cache writes gives **USD4.001485**. Cache breakdown,
auxiliary title-call usage and a Console billing delta were not captured, so
neither figure is an exact all-inclusive bill or enforced ceiling. Pricing:
[official Anthropic documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing).

The portable fixture bundle has eight deterministic regression checks, including
changed source/hash, missing/unlisted file and unsafe-path failures; DOCX text
equality and repeat-byte checks cover sixteen generated documents. The upstream
validator covers 170 workflows, 28 column files and ten packs in this checkout.
These are schema/integrity checks, not behavioral reliability tests.
